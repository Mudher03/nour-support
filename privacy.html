Nour Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026.

Nour teaches Islam through short lessons, prayer times, a Quran reader and an optional AI chat. We collect as little as we can. This policy explains what leaves your phone, where it goes and why.

What stays on your phone

Your lesson progress, streak, gems, settings, the name you type in, bookmarks and prayer checklist are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, a copy of your progress is also kept in your own iCloud account, so it comes back if you reinstall Nour or move to a new iPhone, and stays the same across your devices. Apple holds that copy for you; we never receive it.

Location

With your permission, Nour uses your location on your phone to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction, and keeps it up to date while the Qibla compass is open so the compass can point from true north. We do not receive or store it. To show your city's name, your phone asks Apple Maps for the place name at those coordinates. If you choose your city by name instead, what you type is sent to Apple Maps to find it. Apple's privacy policy covers both requests.

Prayer lock (optional)

If you turn on the prayer lock, you choose apps for it with Apple's Screen Time picker. Apple keeps that choice private: Nour only receives anonymous tokens and cannot see which apps or websites you picked. Nothing about the lock leaves your phone.

Accounts and friends

This version of Nour has no accounts and no friends feature: there is nothing to sign up for, and we hold no email addresses. If accounts are added later, this policy will be updated first to say exactly what they store.

Age check for the AI chat

The first time you open the AI chat, Nour asks Apple's age range service whether you are 18 or over. Apple shares only that answer, never your birthday, and only if you (or your parent, for a child account) agree. If it can't answer, Nour asks you directly. The answer is kept on your phone. For a reader under 18, a parent can allow the chat in Settings with a parent code; the code is stored on the phone only as a scrambled value (a hash), and is never sent anywhere.

AI chat (optional, with your permission)

The chat only works after you agree to it, and for readers under 18 only if a parent has allowed it. Each message you send, and any photo you attach, goes through Nour's server to Google's Gemini AI so it can write a reply. Nour's server does not store your chats; it only uses your connection's IP address briefly to limit how many requests one device can send. Google may keep what you send, use it to improve its services, and people at Google may review it, under Google's own terms. Please don't share private details in the chat. You can withdraw permission in Settings at any time.

If you speak instead of typing, your phone turns your voice into text using Apple's speech recognition before anything is sent.

Listening to pages

When you tap "Listen" on a reading page, or hear the pronunciation of a dua, a hadith or one of the Names of Allah, that text is sent through Nour's server to Google's text-to-speech service to create the audio, which is then saved on your phone. The text is the app's content, not anything you wrote.

Quran, tafsir and hadith text

Quran verses, translations and pronunciation guides, and the chapters of the hadith collections, are downloaded from a public content network (jsDelivr). Quran recitations, in the Quran reader and in memorisation practice, are recorded recitations downloaded from everyayah.com, by the reciter you choose, and memorisation practice downloads each verse's words and their pronunciation from Quran.com. The tafsir (Al-Mukhtasar, by the Tafsir Center for Quranic Studies) is built into the app. As with any web request, those services see your IP address.

Usage statistics (off unless you turn them on)

If you switch on "Share anonymous usage stats", Nour sends short event records such as "lesson completed" with the lesson's ID and score. They carry a random ID created on your phone, not your name, email or account, and we use them only to see which parts of the app help people. You can switch this off in Settings at any time.

What we don't do

We show no ads. We don't track you across other apps or websites. We don't sell or share your data for advertising.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything personal stays on your phone and, if iCloud is on, in your own iCloud account. Deleting the app removes the copy on your phone; you can remove the iCloud copy in your iPhone's Settings under your name, then iCloud. Usage statistics, if you turned them on, carry only a random ID and cannot be traced back to you; switching them off stops any more being sent.

Children

Nour's lessons are written for everyone, including children and new Muslims. The AI chat is the one part for adults: it stays off for readers under 18 unless a parent allows it, as described above. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children; nothing a child does in Nour is sent to us, apart from anonymous usage statistics if they are switched on.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
