Common questions

My progress is missing on a new phone. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud on, then open Nour and wait a moment: your progress comes back from your own iCloud.

Prayer times look wrong. In the Prayer tab, check the location, the calculation method and the Asr school. Each prayer can also be moved a few minutes to match your mosque.

The AI chat says it's for adults. The chat is for readers 18 and over. A parent can allow it for a younger reader in Settings, under AI chat for under-18s.

The prayer lock won't let me in. Tick the prayer off on the Prayer tab, or press "Unlock anyway" twice on the lock screen.
